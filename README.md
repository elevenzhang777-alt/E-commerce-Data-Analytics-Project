# E-commerce Data Analytics Project

## Overview

End-to-end analysis of 1M e-commerce orders to identify revenue drivers, customer behavior, product performance, profitability, and delivery issues.

## Business Questions

Which products and categories drive revenue and profit?
How does repeat purchasing contribute to overall revenue?
Which customer segments generate the most value?
What factors are associated with late delivery and poor reviews?
Where are the main opportunities for improving profitability?

## Analysis

SQL: Data cleaning, validation, joins, CTEs, window functions, aggregations, KPI analysis, and analytical datasets.
Python: EDA, customer segmentation, RFM analysis, product/category analysis, and statistical exploration.
Power BI: Data modelling, DAX measures, KPI dashboards, slicers, and interactive business analysis.

## Key Insights

$983.14M product sales across 1M orders
$251.50M gross profit with a 25.58% gross margin
Repeat customers generated 95.5% of revenue
Electronics accounted for approximately 74.5% of revenue
The top 10% of products generated approximately 66.7% of revenue
Late delivery rate was 9.86%, with delivery performance strongly associated with review scores

## Workflow

Raw Data → SQL Cleaning & Analysis → Analytical Layer → Python EDA → Power BI → Business Report

## Tools

MySQL · Python · Pandas · Power BI · Excel
